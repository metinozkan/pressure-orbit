Game access

Your first three gameplay runs are free. Once used, one additional run becomes available after a three-minute wait. Learning and help remain available. Availability may be interrupted by maintenance or technical problems. You can also choose to watch an optional rewarded ad for one extra run.

Last Pilot multiplayer

Opening a 2–4-player room requires a paid weekly Orbit Plus subscription or lifetime purchase, or access explicitly granted by us. This mode has no free host trial, free-turn allowance, cooldown entry or ad entry. Invited players may join a paid host’s room for free. All players use their own phones. Three mistakes eliminate a player; the last remaining wins. If all remaining players lose their final life together, they enter a one-life final duel. A technical interruption may restart a question or cancel the match. Room wins are separate from solo global rankings. The existing free-turn rules apply to solo play.

Fair play

In missions, a player who runs out of lives near the end may revive once with one extra life by watching an optional ad or through premium, which includes one free extra life per mission run. A revived run earns at most one star and is not submitted to the global ranking. Premium removes ads and gives no score multipliers. Competitive scores are calculated from actual input events and verified on the server; modified clients, fake inputs or abuse may be rejected and accounts restricted.
