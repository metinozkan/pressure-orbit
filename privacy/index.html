Privacy policy

This policy describes personal data used by PRESSURE! Orbit, operated by Metin Özkan. It covers the game and this support website. Contact: metin.ozkn1@gmail.com.

What the game processes

A guest or linked account identifier; optional email and pilot name; game progress, stars, preferences and scores; actual input events used to verify competitive runs; and connection/security data needed to operate and protect the service. Your visible pilot name and verified score appear in global rankings. Never use personal information as a public pilot name.

Purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment. RevenueCat processes purchase status, store transactions and an app user identifier to provide and restore premium access. We do not receive full payment-card details. Purchases do not require a game account. RevenueCat uses its own anonymous purchase identifier, separate from the Supabase game identity. Restore purchases with the same store account; this does not restore unlinked game progress or transfer purchases between Apple and Google. In updated versions, the existing anonymous purchase identifier may be associated with your game pilot for access support. This association does not change or transfer store purchases and does not require registration.

Why data is used

To save and synchronize progress, verify scores, prevent abuse, provide or restore purchases, respond to support and operate the app. Sound, vibration and language preferences are saved on your device. The free-run allowance is also stored on your device.

Service providers

The game uses our self-hosted Supabase services for identity and game data, RevenueCat for purchase management, and Apple or Google for store purchases. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process connection information such as IP addresses in its hosting logs. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Private multiplayer rooms

In versions with Last Pilot, other room participants can see your pilot name, lives, connection status, elimination and match result. The server processes press/release events to judge shared questions and stores private match results separately from solo rankings. To verify paid hosting, a private random purchase identifier can be linked to your existing guest pilot and RevenueCat purchases. Linking is explicit; restoring a purchase is a separate action. Registration is not required. Deleting your pilot also deletes its private multiplayer identity mapping and results, without cancelling store subscriptions.

Optional ranking notifications

In versions with this feature, notifications are optional. With your permission, we associate an installation identifier and push token with your pilot, language, time zone and ranking-alert history to notify you when real players pass you. Expo Push Service and Apple APNs or Google FCM process the token and notification payload, including an opaque pilot identifier, for delivery. This is not advertising tracking. Turn notifications off in the app or device settings. Deleting your pilot removes its server-side notification records. These identifiers are not public. Expo privacy: https://expo.dev/privacy

Advertising and tracking

The app offers optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob, which may use Unity Ads as a mediation partner. You can choose to watch an ad to receive one extra run when free runs are used up, or one extra life near the end of a mission; declining or closing it does not stop the ordinary free-run timer. Ads are never shown automatically and Orbit Plus members see no ads. Advertising services may process device or advertising identifiers where permitted, IP address and approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. We request non-personalized ads and do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission; this still involves data processing. Where required, Google's consent form appears before ad requests. Manage available choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices. We do not sell personal data or add advertising cookies to this website. Google privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · Unity privacy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Storage, security and deletion

Game data is kept as needed to provide your account and its progress, comply with applicable obligations and protect the service. Delete your guest or linked game account from Settings → Change your pilot name → Delete My Pilot in app versions offering this option, or contact support on this website. Backups and legally required security or purchase records may be retained for a limited purpose after account deletion. We use technical controls but cannot promise absolute security.

Your choices

You can choose a pilot name, adjust preferences, request access/correction/deletion and manage purchases through the store. Rights depend on applicable law. Children should ask a parent or guardian before purchasing. The game is entertainment, not a health assessment.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or its providers change. The date on this page indicates the latest version. Contact us for questions about data handling.
